---
title: 3. Send your first notification
description: Register your phone, send a test, then publish a post and watch it arrive.
---

About 5 minutes, and this is the fun one.

<Callout type="warn">
	Turn on **Dev mode** first. With it on, nothing you send can reach real users — only devices you
	have flagged as development devices. Leave it on until you are done experimenting.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>

### Turn on Dev mode

**MrDemonWolf → Push Settings → Dev mode**, tick it, save.

</Step>

<Step>

### Register your phone

Open the MrDemonWolf app on your phone and make sure notifications are allowed. The app registers
itself with your site the moment you enable them.

Now go to **MrDemonWolf → Devices** in wp-admin.

You should see: one device listed, with its platform and model.

<Callout type="warn">
	Nothing listed? The app has not reached your site. Check that the app is pointing at the right
	site address, and that notification permission was actually granted rather than dismissed.
</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Flag it as a dev device

In the device's row, turn on the **Dev** toggle. With Dev mode on, this is now the only device that
will receive anything.

</Step>

<Step>

### Send a test

Go to **MrDemonWolf → Send**. Fill in a title and a body — anything at all. Leave the target as
**All devices** (Dev mode narrows it to your phone anyway).

Click **Send Now**.

You should see: the notification arrive on your phone within a few seconds.

</Step>

<Step>

### Now try it with an image

Back on the **Send** screen, put an image URL in the **Image URL** field, or click **Choose Image**
and pick something from your media library. Send again.

You should see: the notification arrive with the picture attached.

<Callout type="info">
	Nothing appeared on iPhone but the text came through? That is expected unless your app build
	includes a Notification Service Extension. Apple requires one before an image can be attached to a
	push. Android shows it with no app changes at all.
</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Make it automatic

**MrDemonWolf → Push Settings**, turn on **Auto-notify on publish** and **Use featured image**, then
save.

Now publish a post with a featured image. The notification goes out by itself, with the featured
image attached.

Every post also gets a **MrDemonWolf** box in the editor sidebar, where you can change the wording
just for that post, or stop it notifying at all.

</Step>

<Step>

### Turn Dev mode off when you are ready

Once you are happy, go back to **Push Settings** and untick **Dev mode**. Real devices start
receiving from the next send onward.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Why the image sometimes does not appear

The image is downloaded by the phone itself, so it has to be reachable from anywhere:

- It must be **HTTPS**. Plain `http://` is refused by iOS and the plugin rejects it before sending.
- It must be on a **public address**. A staging site behind a login, `localhost`, or a `.local`
  address cannot be reached from a phone on mobile data.
- Keep it under about **1 MB**. iOS allows up to 10 MB, but Android quietly drops large images.

The plugin checks the first two before sending, so an unusable URL is refused rather than producing
a notification with a silently missing picture.
